About Fleet AI

Fleet AI

An unplanned repair affects more than a vehicle. It changes the driver's day, the shop's schedule, and the commitments a fleet made to its customers.

Make the warning useful.

Fleet AI brings vehicle signals and operating history into the maintenance conversation. The aim is earlier visibility into developing issues, backed by evidence that an operator or technician can review.

The work begins with reliable data collection. It continues through careful modeling, clear explanations, and a record of what actually happened at the vehicle.

Physics and history
Use physical relationships and observed behavior together. A model score is a reason to investigate, not a substitute for diagnosis.
Field evidence
Evaluate recommendations against inspections and confirmed repair outcomes. Keep simulation results separate from real-fleet performance.
People in control
Drivers, fleet managers, and qualified technicians make operating and maintenance decisions. Fleet AI supports their judgment.
