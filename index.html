What Fleet AI is and is not

Clear expectations help fleets adopt the platform with confidence.

Fleet AI is
  • A decision-support platform
  • A learning system that progressively adapts to your fleet
  • Designed to reduce surprises and improve planning
Fleet AI is not
  • A replacement for mechanics
  • A fully autonomous controller
  • A magic prediction engine

Operator-first AI principles
  • AI advises, humans decide
  • Explanations included in recommendations
  • No black-box actions
  • Oversight always remains with operators
Who Fleet AI is for
  • Fleets seeking predictability and fewer surprises
  • Operators open to piloting and iteration
  • Teams focused on uptime and compliance readiness
Who it is not ideal for
  • Fleets expecting instant automation
  • Teams unwilling to test or iterate
  • Organizations looking for hands-off control
What happens when something goes wrong?
  • AI does not take autonomous action
  • Operators remain in control of decisions
  • Alerts escalate, not override
  • Human review is always possible
Data and trust
  • Fleet data is isolated per customer
  • No cross-fleet data sharing
  • Data is not sold
  • Intelligence remains fleet-specific
