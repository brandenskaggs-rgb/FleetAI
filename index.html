What Fleet AI actually is

A predictive maintenance platform — not another dashboard

Fleet AI plugs into your vehicles via OBD-II or J1939 and continuously reads sensor data across the entire drivetrain — engine, thermal, fuel, electrical. ML models learn what normal looks like for each specific vehicle, then flag when something starts drifting off baseline.

The output isn't a chart or another alert to ignore. It's a prioritized queue of which vehicles need attention, why, and how soon — delivered to the people who can actually do something about it.

How the operating loop works

Four steps from raw telemetry to your team taking action.

01

Collect

Fleet AI reads sensor data directly from vehicle interfaces via OBD-II or J1939 — continuously, with offline buffering when connectivity drops.

02

Analyze

ML models compare live readings against each vehicle's established baseline and flag signals that indicate developing mechanical drift.

03

Prioritize

Signals get ranked by urgency — what needs immediate attention, what needs monitoring, and what can safely wait — into a clear operator queue.

04

Act

Operators and leadership see the same prioritized picture. Fleet AI recommends the timing and reasoning — your team makes the calls.
