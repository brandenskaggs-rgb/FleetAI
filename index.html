Serious infrastructure for serious fleet environments

Fleet AI is built to operate like infrastructure — not a demo app with AI branding on top of it.

  • Multi-protocol telemetry: OBD-II for light-duty, J1939 for heavy-duty
  • Offline-first architecture — buffers and replays when coverage drops
  • Org-scoped data isolation — no cross-tenant data mixing
  • Deployment security built in, not bolted on after launch
