How the prediction layer works

Vehicle baselines, not generic thresholds.

A healthy Freightliner Cascadia does not behave like a Transit van, and a city route does not behave like long-haul. Fleet AI starts with pretrained fleet priors, then calibrates to each customer's real telemetry.

The model tracks coolant variance, voltage sag, RPM instability, oil pressure behavior, load correlation, fuel activity, fault-code patterns, and missing-signal quality. When the pattern moves outside that unit's expected baseline, it appears in the Risk Queue with an evidence trail.
