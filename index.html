How Fleet AI runs your predictive spine

Each loop is engineered for industrial fleets: OBD-II for light duty, 6/9-pin J1939 for heavy duty, Cloudflare Tunnel for ingress, and intelligence that publishes executive-ready directives.

01
Instrument every asset

Certified OBD-II and 9-pin J1939 harnesses stream RPM, torque, voltage, fuel trim, and aftertreatment vitals. Device health is authenticated per vehicle so ghost units never enter the feed.

02
Secure ingress + tunneling

Cloudflare Tunnel and mutual TLS move payloads into Fleet AI without exposing roadside hardware. Every packet is time-signed, buffered offline, and replayed once the tunnel is back online.

03
Intelligence & reconciliation

Signal cleaners reconcile dual-source sensors, merge OBD-II + J1939 frames, and feed the reliability brain. Models cross-check the whole fleet to spot anomalies before they become downtime.

04
Directed actions + accountability

Operators receive inspection windows, cost exposure, and compliance notes. Leadership receives the same verdict packaged for board decks, so decisions align from the shop floor to the capital stack.

Command scope and non-negotiables

Fleet AI behaves like serious infrastructure—tight remit, no mystery automation, documented boundaries.

Fleet AI owns
  • Predictive maintenance intelligence tied to RPM/MAP/torque reality.
  • Multi-protocol ingestion (OBD-II, CAN, J1939) with sensor arbitration.
  • Secure session management through Cloudflare Tunnel + org-scoped tokens.
  • Executive-ready reporting that mirrors the operator console.
Fleet AI will not do
  • Autonomous control overrides or remote actuation.
  • Cross-tenant data blending or selling telemetry to third parties.
  • Shadow AI explanations—every alert cites the data that triggered it.
  • Unreviewed updates; all changes are versioned and logged.

Deployment readiness checklist
  • Fleet has mixed light/heavy duty assets that need one predictive system.
  • Ops can dedicate at least one lead tech or reliability manager.
  • Leadership wants telemetry they can take straight into investor decks.
  • Security expects Cloudflare Tunnel, MFA, and auditable access.
Not a fit if
  • You expect a black-box autopilot or push-button fixes.
  • Vehicles cannot expose OBD-II, CAN, or J1939 data.
  • Leadership is unwilling to pilot on real metal before scale.
  • Security requires data residency outside the United States.
Failure protocol
  • Device health monitoring escalates if a tablet, dongle, or harness falls offline.
  • Alerts include next-best data sources and how long buffers can hold.
  • Ops receives runbooks for roadside swaps and post-incident analysis.
  • No recommendation is issued without sensor integrity confidence.
Data stewardship
  • Org-scoped encryption keys, no shared databases, no shadow analytics.
  • Tunnel-only ingress prevents direct exposure of operator dashboards.
  • Access logs are exportable for SOC 2 / ISO 27001 evidence.
  • Telemetry is deleted on contract termination per SLA.
