Vehicle baselines

Every vehicle gets its own normal range.

A Class 8 Cascadia running long-haul does not behave like a Transit cargo van on city routes. Fleet AI builds an individual baseline for each unit based on how it actually operates — not industry averages.

When a vehicle starts drifting outside its own range — in coolant behavior, voltage, RPM, oil pressure, or fuel — it surfaces in the risk queue with the specific readings that triggered it.

Risk queue

Your shop gets a list, not a wall of data.

Fleet AI doesn't send every alert — it ranks vehicles by urgency, available service window, and likely impact. Your team sees what to pull first and why, with the evidence behind each call.

Dispatchers, drivers, and the shop all work from the same queue. A vehicle flagged today becomes a scheduled work order before it becomes a road call.
