Understand the change.
Then decide what comes next.

A temperature reading alone is not a diagnosis. Fleet AI looks for changes in context, with physics checks, statistical models, and a vehicle's operating history.

Missing signals stay missing. Synthetic priors are not proof of a real-world failure. Confidence and supporting evidence matter as much as the score.

Read the signals
A compatible connection supplies available OBD-II or J1939 readings. Hardware, vehicle configuration, and permissions determine coverage.
Build the context
Historical trends and each vehicle's learned baseline help distinguish a changing operating condition from an isolated reading.
Prioritize maintenance
Review risk, fault evidence, and data quality before assigning an inspection or service task.
Record the outcome
Keep inspection and repair findings alongside the history. Confirmed outcomes are necessary to validate predictions.
