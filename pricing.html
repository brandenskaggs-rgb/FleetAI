Before you start

Does the tablet alone connect to the truck?

Not necessarily. A compatible gateway or adapter and a supported data interface are required. We check the existing hardware before planning installation.

Does this replace our ELD during the pilot?

The initial pilot can focus on maintenance monitoring alongside your existing process. Do not replace required ELD equipment based only on a Fleet AI pilot.

Are breakdowns guaranteed to be predicted?

No. Coverage, operating history, and confirmed outcomes affect what the system can assess. Predictions are advisory and need practical validation.
